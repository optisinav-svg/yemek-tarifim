# Research assets

The initial recipe-card imagery was sourced from an image search for Turkish recipe photography. The selected local assets are copies of the following search results:

| Project asset | Search result | Source |
|---|---|---|
| `assets/images/recipes/mercimek-kofte.jpg` | Turkish lentil meatballs | A Kitchen in Istanbul — https://vidarbergum.com |
| `assets/images/recipes/manti.jpg` | 22 Turkish Recipes | Olive Magazine — https://www.olivemagazine.com |
| `assets/images/recipes/zeytinyagli.jpg` | Wholesome, Easy Turkish Food Ideas | Ozlem's Turkish Table — https://ozlemsturkishtable.com |

These images are being used as local development assets for the recipe-card prototype. Before public release, licensing/usage permissions should be verified or replaced with owned/licensed imagery.
